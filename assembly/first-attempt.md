1/28/2026 tried to assemble the first "production" blind (IE blind that will operate in my house).

Some notable issues, which required STL changes; and BOM changes. 

6mm grub screws is way too long, I thought they would feed out the back. 3mm is fine thats the length of my inserts.

Extra plastic behind inserts can melt and get in the way. 

Press is needed for motor gear. 

1/29/2026: 

Getting the rope UNDER the parts is difficult,  a small channel would help. 
The tops of the pieces being closed means you need to assemble everything then slide it in. 
Modified the end stop to be open so they can be placed in any order.
Added rope reliefs to things that needed it. 

Other than that assembly was completed. Saying it went smooth feels like a bit of a stretch. 
I did things 'backward' IE the motor orientation drives everything. 
And then even knowing that I still flipped it.

Then actually getting the pieces into the blind was a bit of an annoying thing. 
Some of the set screws were too far and made it difficult to insert into the blind. 
Then some of the unset set screws (motor) had plastic preventing me from putting the screw in. 

Ultimately I completed my goal but it was a bit of a slog.
