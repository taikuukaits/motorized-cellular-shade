I've tried to reduce the number of components, preferring M2.5 fasteners and M2.5 heat inserts (and grub screws).
That said, the end stop mounting holes are too small for 2.5 so they use M2 bolts and M2 nuts
The Lead Screw holes are too large so they use M3 bolts and heat sets. It can't use nuts due to clearance. If your lead screw holes are threaded you don't need M3 heat sets. Theoretically you could buy Tr8x2 nuts with M3 threads but at ~1.5 per lead screw its not worth it since heat sets are cheap (~6.5 for 100 = 25 blinds, or 37.5 in nuts)
Lastly the motor although it uses M2.5 it needs a counter sink screw. The rest are bolts. 

Also generally I found it cheaper to buy assortments; ie buying an M2 assortment got you 20 12mm M2 screws and 200 nuts for 6.59 but buying 100 12mm M2 might cost 5.99. So if you are building less than 5 blinds, the assortment makes sense. You will pay more on Amazon vs AliExpress but AliExpress is way cheaper; for example you can get the same M2 12mm 50pcs for 2.24 shipped (or 1 on sale). So if you are willig to wait you can spend significantly less. For some items, even McMaster can be cheapest - M2 100 pcs 12mm are 4.46 but you will need to pay shipping.

Hardware I used the price I paid. If I paid shipping, its included. You might be able to save by finding alternate suppliers. 

In some cases I used assortments. When I used an assortment I took cost divided by the total number which is not really accurate. If you need to build 10 of these at 1 screw each and the assortment has 9 screws you are going to need two assortments even if thats the only screw you needed 10. This could artificially inflate the cost of my fasteners. 

When buying a lead screw, make sure you get Tr8x2 because we want maximum rotations for minimum travel distance of the lead screw. If you get a T8x8 for example you will likely exceed the avaiable space in the shade while trying to roll it up. 

I found Amazon cheapest for lead screws but did look at bulkman3d for 1.99 a lead screw but really high ($80) shipping. At some point it would make sense. I needed around 13 so its close at 145.12 but Amazon was still cheaper at 120.93 for all. 

For heat set insert sizing, best I could find was prusa: https://www.prusa3d.com/product/heat-set-inserts-m2-5-standard-100-pcs/
