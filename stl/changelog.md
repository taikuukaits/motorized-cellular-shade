-  Jan 28 2026

Boxed End Stop 1.0.2: Shrank the 'foot' to make more room for the grub screw and insert. While inserting an heat set insert it 'clogged up'. I think just lowering the amount of excess plastic should solve this.

Shaft Bearing Holder V1.0.1: Same clogging, lowered foot by 1mm. Also recommend to print a mirrored one. 

Lead Block Barrier 1.0.1: Switched to M2.5 and nuts. The M3 screws while they have no slack, their inserts are just too close to the edge. Heat sets are too risky this close to the edge. M2.5 with nuts is a pretty tight fit, but also slack (backlash) is not really a problem (even if there was a lot) due to the endstop design. This also eliminates M3 from the equation. 

Top Electronic Stack 1.0.2: didnt quite have proper clearance for D1 mini. Using countersunk screws here (already in the motor) because of clearance issues with the USB port (and it looks nicer).
